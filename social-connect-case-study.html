Social Connect

1. Context

I designed the Social Connect app and vision was to create a privacy focused and minimalist platform that allows users to share real moments with their close circle. The project aimed to move away from noisy, algorithm driven social media and instead provide a calm, authentic, and secure space for meaningful connections.

Note: This is a self-made project where ChatGPT acted as a client to help me practice UI/UX design skills.

2. Problem Statement

According to the client, today’s social media platforms are noisy, algorithm driven, and focused on followers rather than real connections. There is currently no simple system where users can share privately within a small, trusted circle of friends.

Analysis (Competitive Analysis)

In my research, I compared WhatsApp and Instagram. Instagram’s reels and stories are endless and noisy due to algorithms, while WhatsApp statuses are limited to real friends but lack engaging update features.

This showed a gap for a platform that offers both privacy and meaningful sharing.

Target Audience Pain Points

11. Conclusion

The main challenge I faced was building a fully privacy focused app in a space already dominated by existing social platforms, especially Meta’s apps.

Most users are familiar with and trust these platforms, making them less likely to switch.

Additionally, I had to ensure my design was original to avoid copyright issues.

The outcome I reached was to combine selected features of two popular Meta apps, but with a unique style using different fonts and colors.

At the same time, I kept a familiar layout so users can easily navigate, since they are already familiar with these apps.

In this project, I learned how to analyze and break down existing competitive systems during research. I also discovered that most users prefer privacy-focused solutions over noisy, time-consuming platforms.

12. Scope of Improvements

Call Feature Improvement

The app includes call and video call features similar to WhatsApp, ensuring users can connect in real time.

However, the interface is redesigned with a fresh and minimal UI, giving a more private and user-friendly experience.

Status Feature Improvement

The app provides a built-in Status Saver that allows users to save their friends’ shared moments directly within the app.

This makes it easier to revisit important updates without needing third party tools, while keeping everything private and secure.
