DOCWISE AI Smart Document Summarizer

1. Context

I designed DOCWISE, an AI powered document summarizer. The vision was to create a simple, clean platform that helps students, writers, and researchers save time by turning long documents into short, meaningful summaries in seconds.

Note: This is a university FYP project where I complete my this project with 2 class mate and contribution is from ideas to frontend code and I also lead the group.

2. Problem Statement

According to the client, reading and understanding long documents is time consuming. There is currently no simple tool where users can upload a PDF and instantly get a clean, accurate summary without distractions or technical complexity.

4. Project Goal

To build a simple, distraction free AI summarizer that allows users to upload any PDF and get a clear summary instantly — without any technical knowledge required.

5. Research Part

Persona: Student / Content Writer

Age: 18–30
Tech Level: Medium (regular users of Google Docs, ChatGPT, Word)

Pain Points: Wastes hours reading full documents. Needs quick understanding for assignments or work.

Values: Speed, simplicity, accuracy.

Analysis (Competitive Analysis)

I compared existing tools like ChatPDF and Adobe AI Summary. Most require login, subscriptions, or have noisy interfaces. This showed a gap for a free, clean, and instant summarizer with no friction.

Target Audience

Age: 16–35
Demographics: students, writers, researchers
Tech Level: medium

Target Audience Pain Points

  • Wastes too much time reading full documents
  • Existing tools require signup or payment
  • Complex interfaces feel overwhelming
  • Needs clean output that is easy to copy or download

Use Case Analysis

Created scenarios in Maze: uploading a document, reading the summary, downloading result, and navigating saved summaries.

6. Brainstorming & Ideation

Your Approach

After client research, I found that most summarizer tools either have poor UI or require too many steps. I focused on a 3 step process: Upload → Summarize → Download.

Possible Solutions

Combine the simplicity of a notes app with the power of AI, remove all unnecessary steps, and deliver results on a single clean screen.

9. Conclusion

The main challenge was designing an AI tool that feels trustworthy and simple for non technical users. The outcome was a clean 3 step flow with a familiar layout. I learned how to design for clarity — where less UI means more trust.

10. Scope of Improvements

Multi-format Support

Currently PDF only. Future version should support Word, TXT, and PPT files.
